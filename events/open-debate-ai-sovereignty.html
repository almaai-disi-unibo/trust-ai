AI sovereignty: independence, interdependence and democratic control

Hybrid formatProgramme in development

The question

What does sovereign AI mean? Is complete independence technically or politically possible? Who should exercise control, and can AI sovereignty be democratic?

Format

Contrasting positions will be represented fairly by invited speakers and, where useful, prepared groups of students or early-career researchers. A follow-on working group will identify research, policy or practical implications.

Experimental component

An AI system may be included as a respondent to examine how it frames the issue, which assumptions it reproduces and how its contributions compare with human perspectives.
