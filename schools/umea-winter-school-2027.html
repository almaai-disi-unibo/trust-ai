AI, Ethics and Society Winter School

Umeå, SwedenUsually held in March

About the school

Umeå University’s annual school focuses on AI, ethics and society. It is primarily designed for PhD students, while also welcoming non-academic participants and partners.

TRUST-AI Founders may contribute as lecturers and may encourage their students and collaborators to participate.
