About the initiative

Responsible, Inclusive, Compliant and Sustainable AI, made practical through collective expertise.

TRUST-AI is a distributed, interdisciplinary and collaborative research initiative.

// Vision

An international environment for design and build an AI we can trust.

TRUST-AI supports research, innovation, education and policy for the responsible and sustainable development, deployment and governance of AI.

// Mission

Connecting complementary perspectives.

We connect leading research groups with public institutions, industry, civil society and affected communities across the scientific, technical, regulatory, environmental and societal dimensions of AI. Through foundational and applied research, education, innovation and policy initiatives, we translate knowledge into responsible AI practices with tangible societal impact.
