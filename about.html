About the initiative

Responsible AI, made practical through collective expertise.

TRUST-AI is a distributed, interdisciplinary and collaborative research initiative.

Vision

An international environment for responsible and sustainable AI.

TRUST-AI supports research, innovation, education and policy for the responsible and sustainable development, deployment and governance of AI.

Mission

Connecting complementary perspectives.

We connect leading research groups across scientific, technical, regulatory, environmental and societal dimensions of AI, fostering foundational and applied research, education, innovation and policy initiatives with societal impact.

Across the AI lifecycle

One initiative, complementary capabilities.

Our combined expertise supports AI from design to governance, regulation and societal impact.
